UKTL controlled toy benchmark
==============================

Configuration
-------------
Classes: 4 and 10
Tensor shape: 10 x 10 x 10
Retained subspace rank p: 2
Total samples: 400
Train/validation/test split: 50% / 20% / 30%
Seeds: 0, 1, 2
Nyström pivots: 16
Mixture weight mu: 0.05
Epochs: 40
Optimizer: AdamW, lr=3e-3, weight_decay=1e-4

Split
---------------
The clean task is class-balanced. The nuisance task is both class-balanced
and corruption-balanced: clean and corrupted samples are split separately
within every class, so train, validation, and test each contain exactly 50%
corrupted samples. Pivots are initialized from train_x only. Validation is
used only for checkpoint selection. Test data are evaluated only after model
selection.

Results are mean +/- population standard deviation over seeds 0, 1, 2.

Interpretation
--------------
The clean task is a control in which all modes are reliable. Because there is
no nuisance corruption in this condition, Overall test and Clean-subset test
are identical. The nuisance condition is the mechanism-focused test: mode 3
receives a strong, sample-dependent higher-rank component while modes 1 and 2
retain the class-related factors. The larger mean sigma on mode 3 in the
nuisance task is consistent with the intended uncertainty diagnostic, and the
Nuisance-subset test accuracy comparison measures robustness in the designed
stress case.

These are controlled synthetic results, not evidence of universal
superiority. The clean-task gain should not be attributed solely to
uncertainty, because UKTL has additional learned MSN parameters. The
nuisance-mode result is the more direct test of the intended uncertainty
mechanism, but a capacity-matched ablation would be needed to isolate
uncertainty from extra model capacity.
